This project uses the publicly available secondary student survey dataset:

Wickberg Hugerth (2025)
Stockholm University / Swedish National Data Service

DOI: 10.17045/STHLMUNI.28850645

The dataset contains 1,266 responses collected across 9 schools and focuses on secondary students' awareness, attitudes, and use of generative AI for schoolwork.

Dataset Contents

The survey includes information related to:

AI awareness and knowledge
Frequency of AI use
Types of AI use for schoolwork
Attitudes toward acceptable AI use
Perceived school support and guidance regarding AI
